Nexus Bot — Privacy Policy Last Updated: September 27, 2026 This Privacy Policy explains what information Nexus Bot ("the Bot") may collect and how that information is used. 1. Information We May Collect Depending on the features enabled on a Discord server, Nexus Bot may process: Discord User IDs Discord Server IDs Discord Channel and Role IDs Usernames and Display Names Information contained in commands or interactions sent to the Bot Configuration information required for the Bot's features Nexus Bot does not intentionally collect passwords, Discord account tokens, or payment card information. 2. How We Use Information Information processed by Nexus Bot may be used to: Provide and operate Bot features Save server configurations Respond to commands and interactions Provide moderation and administrative features Troubleshoot technical problems Prevent abuse and misuse of the Bot 3. Data Sharing We do not intentionally sell personal information. Information may be processed through Discord and other services required for the operation of specific Bot features. 4. Data Retention Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the Bot's features, maintain configurations, prevent abuse, or meet applicable requirements. Server administrators may request removal of data associated with their server where applicable. 5. Data Security We take reasonable measures to protect information processed by Nexus Bot. However, no online service can guarantee complete security. 6. Children's Privacy Nexus Bot is intended to be used through Discord. Users should comply with Discord's age requirements and applicable laws. 7. Changes to This Privacy Policy This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page. 8. Contact For privacy questions or requests regarding Nexus Bot, please contact the Nexus support team through the official Discord server. Nexus Team
